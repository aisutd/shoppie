About this item

Highlights

  • AN AMAZING MAC AT A SURPRISING PRICE — With an incredibly portable and durable aluminum design, up to 16 hours of battery life,¹ and the A18 Pro chip, MacBook Neo is ready to go wherever school takes you.
  • FOUR STUNNING COLORS. ONE DURABLE DESIGN — Choose from four beautiful colors — Silver, Blush, Citrus, or Indigo — each with a color-coordinated keyboard. And MacBook Neo is made with a durable recycled aluminum enclosure that helps it reach 60 percent recycled content by weight — the most ever in any Apple product.²
  • FLY THROUGH EVERYDAY ASSIGNMENTS — Whether you're cramming for finals, using Apple Intelligence³ to summarize class notes, creating presentations, or even playing the latest Apple Arcade game,⁴ MacBook Neo delivers the performance and AI capabilities you need to get things done.
  • UP TO 16 HOURS OF BATTERY LIFE¹ — MacBook Neo delivers all day battery life, so you can power through from early morning classes to late night study sessions without worrying about plugging in.
  • A VIBRANT 13-INCH DISPLAY⁵ — The gorgeous Liquid Retina display on MacBook Neo supports 1 billion colors, so photos and videos pop and text is crisp for easy reading.
  • A FEAST FOR YOUR SENSES — MacBook Neo comes with a 1080p FaceTime⁶ HD camera, a dual-mic array so your voice sounds focused and clear, and two side-firing speakers to deliver immersive sound. Perfect for video calls with classmates, presenting projects, or streaming your favorite shows.
  • BUILT FOR AI — The AI apps and tools that help you tackle everyday assignments run seamlessly on MacBook Neo. And Apple Intelligence helps you write, express yourself, and get things done effortlessly with groundbreaking privacy protections at every step.
  • MACOS RUNS APPS FAST — All your go-to apps run lightning fast in macOS, including built-in apps like FaceTime and Message.⁷
  • MAC AND IPHONE. EVEN BETTER TOGETHER — Mac and iPhone are amazing on their own. And they're even more amazing when used together. See and control what's on your iPhone right from your Mac.⁷ Start an email on one device and finish it on the other. Snap a photo on your iPhone and see it appear on your Mac. Or make a call without ever reaching for your iPhone.⁸
  • PRIVACY AND SECURITY — MacBook Neo has built-in advanced security protections so you and your data stay safe and protected. FileVault automatically encrypts your files so others can't access them. Find My helps you locate your lost or stolen Mac. And the MacBook Neo model with Touch ID uses your fingerprint to unlock your Mac, authorize payments, and sign in to apps and sites. Plus, every Mac comes with robust antivirus protection and free, automatic security updates to keep your Mac running smoothly and securely.
